# Unsent inquiry: original FEMA coastal-model source holdings

Proposed recipient: FEMA-FMIX@fema.dhs.gov, for routing to FEMA Engineering Library / Archived Flood Data staff.

Subject: Locate original RUNUP, WHAFIS, and CHAMP source listings, microfiche indexes, and delivery media

Hello,

I am researching the original development sources of FEMA's coastal-analysis software. Could you route this inquiry to the custodian of legacy coastal-software records and determine whether the holdings below survive in microfiche, microfilm, contractor-deliverable files, or associated computer media?

The highest-priority item is the original Fortran revision that produced RUNUP2.EXE dated November 6, 1991. The original executable retains these development filenames: RFILES.for, RINPUT.for, RMAIN.for, and RSUBS.for. Its procedure names include RRUFF, SWLINT, SIMPCOMP1, SIMPCOMP2, and SIMPCOMP3. The executable is 119,691 bytes, SHA-256 e0e2b2df60b898d831c5321c9d8a09aa6c9add8333d510d88a396f2e3c2239c8.

Relevant earlier records are:

- Stone & Webster Engineering Corporation, *Manual for Wave Runup Analysis, Coastal Flood Insurance Studies*, November 1981, including Appendix A.6 and its program listing.
- Dewberry & Davis, *Investigation and Improvement of Capabilities for the FEMA Wave Runup Model*, April 1991, including Appendix B and the March 1990 listing, plus later correction sheets, change orders, and PC/Fortran delivery files through November 1991.
- Original WHAFIS development and contractor deliveries from Dames & Moore, October 1980, and Greenhorne & O'Mara revisions through 1989, including printed program listings or computer media. The August 2007 WHAFIS4G.FOR digital distribution is already recovered; earlier independent sources would help establish the lineage.
- CHAMP / Coastal Hazard Analysis Modeling Program versions 1 and 2, approximately 2000–2007: the original Visual Basic project champ.vbp, forms, modules, resources, and build material. An early application preserves C:\champ\champ.vbp; the 2007 version credits Xiaoyi Zhang, Dewberry & Davis. We seek the development deliverables associated with the September 2007 CHAMP 2.0 application.

We already possess the original DOS RUNUP distribution, published 1981 and 1991 report scans, historical CHAMP installers, and the 2007 WHAFIS source. The 2024 RUNUP reconstruction does not answer the missing historical-source question.

If direct source files are unavailable, please identify relevant catalog/accession numbers, series, boxes, fiche envelopes/cards and frames, contractor-delivery inventories, and any separately stored disks or tapes. For source-bearing fiche, a scan of the title/header, all code frames at sufficient resolution to distinguish Fortran punctuation, and any revision or distribution pages would be useful. Please distinguish a listing from a community flood study that only cites the model.

If another archive or contractor is the custodian, a referral or transfer/accession reference would help. Please advise on availability and any research or reproduction fees before starting chargeable work; this inquiry does not authorize charges.

Thank you.
