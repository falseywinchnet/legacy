Documentation

How to order U.S. Geological Survey printed documentation

  • Franz, D.D., and Melching, C.S., 1997, Full Equations (FEQ) model for the solution of the full, dynamic equations of motion for one-dimensional unsteady flow in open channels and through control structures: U.S. Geological Survey Water-Resources Investigations Report 96-4240, 258 p.

    Browse the FEQ documentation or retrieve and print one of the following:

    • FEQ documentation, Portable Document Format (PDF) (3.51MB)
    • FEQ documentation, PostScript, gzip-compressed for UNIX (992K)
    • FEQ documentation, PostScript, self-extracting DOS executable (1MB)

  • Franz, D.D., and Melching, C.S., 1997, Full Equations Utilities (FEQUTL) model for the approximation of hydraulic characteristics of open channels and control structures during unsteady flow: U.S. Geological Survey Water-Resources Investigations Report 97-4037, 205 p.

    Browse the FEQUTL documentation or retrieve and print one of the following:

    • FEQUTL documentation, Portable Document Format (PDF) (3.33MB)
    • FEQUTL documentation, PostScript, gzip-compressed for UNIX (592K)
    • FEQUTL documentation, PostScript, self-extracting DOS executable (608K)
